Placing it in thinkorswim — checklist

  1. Trade tab → choose the ~45-DTE expiration → right-click the short strike → Sell → Vertical (or Iron Condor). Check the long strike is $1 away.
  2. Set a limit price at or near the mid. Confirm the max loss on the order confirmation matches this ticket.
  3. After the fill: create a buy-to-close order for the same spread at the GTC price above, time-in-force GTC.
  4. Add the 21-DTE reminder to your calendar (Google Calendar link, or copy the details into any calendar app).
  5. Log the entry in the journal.
