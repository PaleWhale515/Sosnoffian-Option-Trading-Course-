Premium Selling the Sosnoff Way — Set It, Manage It, Repeat

A self-paced options course for one trader: a Schwab margin account (~$3,000), thinkorswim, Level 2 (Spreads) approval pending, no screen time during the workday. The goal is a routine you can follow in the 8:30–10:00 AM CT window: select a trade, set it, manage it intermittently, repeat. Learning first, earning second.

Attribution labels

Every principle is labeled so you know where it comes from:

  • Sosnoff's view — Tom Sosnoff's stated view, with a link to where he said it.
  • tastylive research — a tastylive study or segment, with a link.
  • Course adaptation — a rule this course made to fit your account, schedule or structures.
  • Unverified — could not be confirmed against a primary source while building the course. See TODO.md.
  • Illustrative — example numbers, not market quotes or statistics.
